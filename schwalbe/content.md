# From the Crypts of Fuchs to the Folds of Schwalbe

*Working Backwards / 03 of 03*

Generative work and live projection, 2023

## Summary

The eye at the moment of flash blindness, projected live during a gig.

## Description

A generative visual work depicting the eye itself at the instant of flash blindness, the damage caused by the light of a nuclear detonation. A dark pupil sits at the centre of dense lines radiating outward in green, black and magenta, overlaid with typographic fragments.

The title maps a journey across the eye. Fuchs' crypts are openings in the iris; Schwalbe's line marks the edge of the cornea. From one to the other is a literal path from iris to cornea, matching the subject of the piece.

Here the traditional rendering in Working Backwards is the live gig visual. The piece was performed as a projection onto and behind the band Pohl, as Atomic Test 1 / Pohl edition, at Strident Nights.

## Materials

- Self-authored generative code
- Live projection
