## About

Patrick Walker is a designer, educator and maker based in Sheffield. He founded the design studio dust, and leads the BA (Hons) Graphic Design course at Sheffield Hallam University.

His practice moves between typography, print and self-authored code: printmaking badly on purpose, setting the parameters for code to explore outcomes. Recent work has moved off the page into live installation, using machinery, cameras and real-time projection.
