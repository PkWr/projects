# dust

*2000–2020*

Patrick Walker, Founder & Creative Director

## Selected projects (in progress)

- [Where Bombs Fell](where-bombs-fell/) 2016

## About

dust was a small creative practice based in Sheffield, founded by Patrick Walker and run from 2000 to 2020. Its work spanned publication design, visual identity, exhibition, letterpress and installation, for clients and collaborators including Museums Sheffield, Corridor 8, Varoom and Manchester United, alongside self-published projects.

Twenty years of dust close one chapter of the practice. PkWr is the next.
