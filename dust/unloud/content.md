# Unloud

Duncan Higgins, 2009

## Summary

Unloud is a publication by the artist Duncan Higgins, made over a two-year collaboration between Higgins and dust, working with the artist-publishers RGAP (Research Group for Artists Publications).

## Description

Set somewhere in north Russia at some point during the last hundred years, Unloud is part fact, part fiction, part ghost story and part love story. The book and its companion film tell the story through the interplay of words and images, in Russian and English, within the landscape where it is set.

The edition comes in a printed kraft folder holding the book and its booklets, a cast object and a copy of the Unloud film, a 30-minute work by Duncan Higgins and Fred Perkins.

It was launched at Site Gallery, Sheffield, on 19 June 2009, alongside the first UK screening of the film. It was the first of a series of RGAP events at Site Gallery, and dust showed a visual and physical response to the process and content explored while the publication was made.
