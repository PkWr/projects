# Broken & Breaking Ground

John Harper, 2008

## Summary

A book and limited boxed edition for artist John Harper's residency with Fermynwoods Contemporary Art, Northamptonshire: a year spent on the Forestry Commission land around Brigstock, challenging the picturesque and the fixed frame through which landscape is usually viewed.

## Description

Broken and Breaking Ground – Ideas taken into and out of a landscape was a yearlong residency commissioned by Fermynwoods Contemporary Art, piloting a programme of artists' residencies at the gallery. It produced monthly bulletins, an exhibition at Spike Island, Bristol, and a book covering the full residency.

dust designed the book and the boxed edition. The book was published by RGAP (Research Group for Artists Publications) and distributed by Cornerhouse, as a hardcover single edition and a limited boxed edition with an additional folio of works.
