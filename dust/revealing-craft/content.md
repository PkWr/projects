# Revealing Craft

India Hobson for Folksy, 2013

## Summary

Revealing Craft is a photobook by the Sheffield photographer India Hobson, published by Folksy, the UK marketplace for handmade work, and designed by dust with Folksy.

## Description

Over 16 months India Hobson photographed 13 makers who sell on Folksy, in their homes and small studios, recording their processes and materials alongside interviews with each of them. The book is about slow work: things that take time, skill and practice to make, often with techniques passed down through generations.

The 72-page book is printed on newsprint and held in a card folio with a die-cut window, its title letters scattered across the cover. It comes with an insert of India's diary from her visits to the makers and a separate fold-out colour photograph.

The makers: James Green, Alison Deegan, Zoe Badger, Debbie Greenaway, Brian Trevaskiss, Sarah Pinney (Northfield Primitives), Agnis Smallwood, Butterscotch & Beesting, Kate Broughton, Kirsty Cross, Harriet Hawkins, Keely Brightmore and Helen Hallows.
