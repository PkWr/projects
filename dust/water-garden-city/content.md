# Water Garden City

Albilad Real Estate, 2008

## Summary

A book introducing Water Garden City, Albilad Real Estate's waterfront development in Bahrain, planned for around 40,000 residents with a masterplan by HOK. Commissioned by M&C Saatchi.

## Description

The book is bound in white cloth with a blind-embossed cover and a ribbon marker. Inside, quotations on water and the desert sit with illustration by Sandrine Pelletier, photograms by Clive Egginton and the plans for the city.
