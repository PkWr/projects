# GD v/x AI

*Research & teaching*

Graphic Design versus, divided by, multiplied by AI

## Summary

A Scholarship of Teaching and Learning project at Sheffield Hallam University, asking whether AI supplants creativity in graphic design, or co-enables and augments it.

## Description

Most classroom use of AI in graphic design means prompting for images, text or layouts. GD v/x AI tests an alternative: students and staff co-developing new AI tools and processes together, moving AI from tool-use into shared creative practice.

Concrete (2025) is its first proof of concept.

At its centre is a motion app that isn't gate-kept behind hardware, digital sovereignty or subscription, and that runs on a fraction of the energy of conventional motion tools. It works on a phone, a desktop and in the Igloo, and it is instant: there is no render time.
