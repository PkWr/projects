# Foxes

*Working Backwards / 01 of 03*

Generative application and photo intaglio, 2023

## Summary

A self-coded generative system producing tens of thousands of foxes, worked backwards into three photo intaglio prints.

## Description

You are looking at 3 fox cubs playing, eyes reflective, under a full moon. The ground is composed of human hair shaved in solidarity with a loved one's journey with leukaemia. This image interprets a narrative of hope and strength taken from a view through a window in treatment.

Foxes is a generative data narrative application designed to bridge the gap between digital output and traditional print technologies. The application generates a new image every minute, using the narrative as a parameter for composition, alongside artist-defined parameters, randomness and combinatorial rules. The live piece can produce up to 106,800 variations.

The generation itself is uncurated. Three outputs were chosen to represent the range of the system rather than a best-of, then worked backwards into physical prints. The prints were produced as part of an induction into the Photo Intaglio process, using solar plates with the support of Specialist Technician, Mike MacGabhann in the Printmaking workshop (TORS).

Foxes was the first of three pieces in Working Backwards (2023), each made as an accompanying invitation to a specific event. It set the pattern the others follow: digital generative process first, traditional or embodied rendering last.

## Materials

- Self-authored generative application
- Photo intaglio on solar plates, three prints
