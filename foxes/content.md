# Foxes

*Working Backwards / 01 of 03*

Generative artwork and etching, 2023

## Summary

A self-coded generative system producing tens of thousands of foxes, worked backwards into three etchings.

## Description

Foxes is a generative system that produces a large body of fox imagery from artist-defined parameters, randomness and combinatorial rules. The live piece can produce up to 106,800 variations.

The generation itself is uncurated. Three outputs were chosen to represent the range of the system rather than a best-of, then worked backwards into physical etchings.

Foxes was the first of three pieces in Working Backwards (2023), each made as an accompanying invitation to a specific event. It set the pattern the others follow: digital generative process first, traditional or embodied rendering last.

## Materials

- Self-authored generative code
- Etching, three prints
